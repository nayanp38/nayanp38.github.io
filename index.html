I'm Nayan, a student at UPenn studying AI and Statistics. My work deals with a combination of conducting research, software development, and entrepreneurship via venture capital.

At Relay, I work on open-world and embodied reasoning models for vision-based products. At Cofounders Capital, I source and evaluate seed-stage B2B software companies, as well as build AI tools for the investment team.

My research background spans Near-Earth Asteroid classification, satellite-image models for 5G planning, and RefuGPT, a multilingual assistant for refugee resettlement guidance.

Outside of work and school, I love to surf, play golf, and watch football (go Panthers!)
